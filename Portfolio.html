Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

01Overview

Privacy is core to how Antify is built. This policy covers how data is handled across the portfolio hub and the full Antify toolset — Antify Zip, Antify Compressor, and Antify Widget.

02Zero uploads, everything runs locally

The tools are built around one idea: your files stay on your device.

  • Antify Zip & Antify Compressor: archiving and image compression run entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
  • Antify Widget: the Windows desktop app runs locally on your machine and doesn't transmit anything off your device.

03What isn't collected

  • No accounts: nothing to register, sign up for, or log into.
  • No file data: file names, contents, and metadata are never read or stored.
  • No analytics or tracking: activity isn't tracked across the web apps or desktop tools.

04Hosting & fonts

The site is hosted on Vercel, which automatically logs standard, non-identifiable server data (like IP address and browser user-agent) for uptime and security. Typography is loaded from Google Fonts (Space Grotesk, Inter, JetBrains Mono), fetched directly by your browser.

05Voluntary support & UPI

Coffee contributions go straight through your device's UPI app to rajveer-nanadikar@fam, with no middleman fees. Bank details, UPI PINs, and financial records are never processed, stored, or accessible to Antify.

06Contact

Questions about this policy or any Antify app — reach out directly.

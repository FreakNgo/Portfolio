Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Antify Studio is run by Rajveer Nanadikar in Kolhapur, Maharashtra, India. This policy covers this website, antifyportfolio.vercel.app. It is short because the site collects very little.

What I collect

This site has no accounts, sign-up forms or contact forms. I don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. The only information I receive is what you choose to send me, for example by emailing antify.web@gmail.com or messaging @freakngo on Instagram.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. The site also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google to fetch them. Both are governed by their own privacy policies.

How I use messages

If you email or message me, I use what you send only to reply to you. I don't sell it or share it with anyone.

Other sites

This site links to my tools, apps and games, and to Instagram. Those open separate sites with their own privacy practices, which this policy does not cover. Antify Share has its own policy at antifyshare.vercel.app/policy.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

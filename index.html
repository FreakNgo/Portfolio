About

        
The idea behind Antify.

        
Antify is Rajveer Nanadikar's personal lab for building small, useful things on the web. Every project starts the same way — something clunky, slow, or missing — and ends as a tool simple enough to use without a manual.

        
No bloat, no unnecessary steps. Just fast, focused tools that do one job well.
